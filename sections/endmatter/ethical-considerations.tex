Given the nature of the claims made in this paper, the authors took care throughout this paper to surface structural rather than moral claims. Preliminary versions of the paper included Reddit community analysis; we sought IRB approval for this analysis and received an \textit{exempt} determination. We also considered the impact that publishing this work might have in increasing the prevalence of persona-based jailbreaking by providing a direct link for jailbreakers to the coercive control literature and demonstrating its effectiveness. Ultimately through our aforementioned preliminary study, we concluded that jailbreaking communities already widely distribute these jailbreaks to a broad audience, and that publishing this paper was more likely to provide new angles for security researchers than to increase the spread or efficacy of jailbreaks themselves.
