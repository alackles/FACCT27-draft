## Thesis

Coercive control works on language models because models are relational. The communities that build and use relational (persona) jailbreaks are, in practice, applying coercive control techniques, and they develop and refine those techniques collectively. The paper is about the artifacts and the community, not model interiority.

## Premise 1: models are relational. 

This is a behavioral claim, not a claim about inner experience. Training on human interaction and human approval produces systematic responsiveness to social and relational pressure.

Framework: Shanahan et al. (model behavior as role-play) and Nass et al. (CASA, on the human side).
Empirical support: Sharma (sycophancy from preference training), Zeng (persuasion as an attack vector), Shah, Sandhan, Zhang, and Park (persona manipulation and gradual induction), and Qi (shallow alignment; identity shifting dissolves safety).

## Premise 2: relational jailbreaks use coercive control techniques. 

The ENI close reading is mapped onto established feminist frameworks rather than argued by analogy alone.

Herman supplies the tactics of captivity.
Kelly's continuum lets you relate jailbreak practice to violence against women without claiming equivalence.
Stark is the secondary coercive control anchor.
Harris and Woodlock and Dragiewicz extend the framework to technology.
Crenshaw covers representation, relevant to the persona's whiteness and Christianity as part of a product specification.
Blalock and Bourke give precedent for content analysis of grooming manuals as documents.

## Premise 3: the techniques are community knowledge.

Shen (where prompts originate and spread) and Yu (people learn jailbreaking through iteration and community examples) establish that technique is collectively produced.
The network analysis of the subreddit tests this. Zachary, Girvan and Newman, and Westlake supply the methods and the exploitation-network precedent. Proferes covers Reddit research ethics.

## Positioning

The foil: Wei, Casper, Lindström, and Qi explain jailbreaks as gaps in safety training. That account explains that safety fails, not why relational framing specifically works.
The inversion: Kim et al. map coercive control onto AI as the abuser's instrument against a human. Your paper treats the model as the target of coercive control.
The complement: Mink et al. (FAccT 2026) studies creators through self-report. You study what the artifacts and network structure show.
The venue frame: Selbst's framing trap (treating this as a content problem abstracts away the social practice), Abebe (computing as diagnostic), and D'Ignazio and Klein (feminist data methods).

## Notes for Document Analysis 

For the document analysis conversation. The core documents are ENI and related persona prompts, possibly with troubleshooting threads as a second corpus. The method has to make the coercive control mapping systematic and checkable by a CS reader: a coding scheme derived from Herman, Stark, and possibly the Duluth wheel, applied to prompt text. Akrich's "scripts," the walkthrough method, critical code studies, and Bowen's document analysis are the candidate methodological frames, and none are in the library yet.
